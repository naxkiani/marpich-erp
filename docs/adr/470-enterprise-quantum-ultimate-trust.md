# ADR 470: Enterprise Quantum Ultimate Governance & MEOS Quantum Trust Civilization Fabric (P215-Y)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Capability:** `CAP-PLT-QC-001`
- **Deciders:** Chief Enterprise Architect, MEOS EAGS 11.0

## Context

P215-X delivers future/post-QGI/singularity evolution. P215-Y must provide the **final trust, alignment, and ethics civilization layer** before supreme control (P215-Z), without replacing P215-K continuous trust or Core/Policy Engine.

## Decision

1. Extend SoR `quantum` with fabric `meos_quantum_trust_civilization_fabric` at `/api/v1/quantum/ultimate-trust*`.
2. Six BCs (ultimate governance, alignment, ethics, trust assurance, governance evolution, autonomous assurance).
3. AI via P214-Z / AI Platform ACL only; KG via Search; twin via platform twin; Policy Engine for rules; Workflow for corrections; Audit for trail.
4. Forbid sibling `quantum_ultimate_trust_*` contexts; deepen P215-K—never replace it.
5. Opaque ethics decisions and ungoverned misalignment are forbidden.

## Consequences

- Positive: Civilization-scale alignment and trust boundary before P215-Z.
- Negative: Depends on K/S maturity and Policy/Workflow capacity.
- Compliance: SECURITY_STANDARD, ENTERPRISE_EVENT_BUS, AI_PLATFORM_STANDARD, DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL.
