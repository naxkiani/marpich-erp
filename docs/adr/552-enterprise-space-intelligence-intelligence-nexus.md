# ADR 552 — Enterprise Space Intelligence Final Intelligence Nexus (P218-Z)

## Status

Accepted — **P218 Series Complete**

## Context

P218-Y established Ultimate Intelligence Governance. P218-Z is the final convergence of Enterprise AI (P214), Quantum (P215), Robotics (P216), Biotechnology (P217) and Space Intelligence (P218) into the MEOS Final Intelligence Nexus — the supreme intelligence control plane.

## Decision

1. SoR remains `space` / `CAP-PLT-SP-001`.
2. Fabric: `meos_final_intelligence_nexus_fabric`.
3. API: `/api/v1/space/intelligence-nexus*`.
4. Six final intelligence layers culminating in Ultimate Intelligence Nexus; control plane + autonomous civilization + orchestration + decision + governance catalogs.
5. All nexus AI via P214-Z / P218-E ACL only — `no_module_local_llm`.
6. Supreme decisions require Workflow + Policy + Identity — never ungated supreme decision; never skip intelligence alignment; never violate human sovereignty; human authority preserved.
7. Ultimate Governance via P218-Y; Singularity via P218-X; Collective SI via P218-W — never replace those platforms.
8. Never replace Core, AI, Quantum, Robotics, Biotechnology, prior P218 fabrics (including P218-Y Ultimate Governance).
9. Marks **P218 series complete** — Enterprise Intelligence Evolution Cycle established.

## Consequences

Positive: unified civilization-scale operating intelligence layer coordinating all MEOS intelligence platforms.  
Negative: nexus operations must stay aligned with Ultimate Governance (P218-Y) and all prior sovereignty/alignment contracts.

## Alternatives rejected

- Sibling `intelligence_nexus` BC outside SoR space.
- Ungated autonomous civilization decisions without human authority.
- Module-local LLM for supreme coordination.
