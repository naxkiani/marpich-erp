# ADR 443 — Enterprise AI Civilization Layer, Collective Intelligence Network & MEOS Future Intelligence Ecosystem Platform (P214-W)

## Status

Accepted

## Context

P214-V introduced the governed AGI cognitive core. MEOS now needs a civilization layer that connects AGI, digital workforce, human expertise, collective learning, and shared knowledge into a living intelligence ecosystem while preserving governance, control, and safety ownership in the earlier P214 layers.

## Decision

1. SoR remains **`ai`** (`CAP-PLT-AI-004`).
2. Fabric: **`meos_collective_intelligence_civilization_fabric`**.
3. API surface: **`/api/v1/ai/aiciv*`**.
4. Seven logical bounded contexts (BC-01–BC-07) remain inside SoR `ai`.
5. P214-W provides the collective-intelligence ecosystem layer, but consumes governance from P214-U, coordination from P214-T, and cognition from P214-V.
6. Knowledge civilization integrates through P214-G and collective decision intelligence through P213 rather than re-owning those substrates.

## Consequences

- P214-X can extend this into post-AGI and singularity-oriented future architecture patterns.
- No module may create a parallel civilization network or alternate collective-intelligence authority outside the `ai` SoR.
