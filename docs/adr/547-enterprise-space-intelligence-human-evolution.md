# ADR 547 — Enterprise Space Intelligence Human Evolution Intelligence (P218-U)

## Status

Accepted

## Context

P218-T established space civilization. P218-U defines human evolution spanning augmentation, cognitive enhancement, neural intelligence and human-machine symbiosis — before Human General Intelligence (P218-V).

## Decision

1. SoR remains `space` / `CAP-PLT-SP-001`.
2. Fabric: `meos_human_evolution_intelligence_fabric`.
3. API: `/api/v1/space/human-evolution*`.
4. Five evolution layers; augmentation + symbiosis + cognitive + neural + governance catalogs.
5. All evolution AI via P214-Z / P218-E ACL only — `no_module_local_llm`.
6. Augmentation approval and neural consent require Workflow + Policy + Identity — never ungated augmentation; never skip human consent; never violate human sovereignty.
7. Bio capability foundations via P217; physical robotic augmentation via P216-Z — never replace those platforms.
8. Never replace Core, AI, Quantum, Robotics, Biotechnology, prior P218 fabrics (including P218-T civilization).
9. Foundation for P218-V.

## Consequences

Positive: responsible human capability expansion foundation for human-machine civilization.  
Negative: evolution operations must stay aligned with Education (P218-S), Civilization (P218-T), Biotechnology (P217) and consent/sovereignty contracts.

## Alternatives rejected

- Sibling `human_evolution` BC outside SoR space.
- Ungated neural interface activation without consent.
- Module-local LLM for cognitive enhancement.
