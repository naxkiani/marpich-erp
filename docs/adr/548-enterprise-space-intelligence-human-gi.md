# ADR 548 — Enterprise Space Intelligence Human General Intelligence (P218-V)

## Status

Accepted

## Context

P218-U established human evolution, augmentation and symbiosis. P218-V defines Human General Intelligence spanning advanced reasoning, collective intelligence, cognitive civilization and knowledge integration — before Collective Super Intelligence (P218-W).

## Decision

1. SoR remains `space` / `CAP-PLT-SP-001`.
2. Fabric: `meos_human_general_intelligence_fabric`.
3. API: `/api/v1/space/human-gi*`.
4. Five Human-GI layers; core + cognitive civilization + collective + reasoning + governance catalogs.
5. All Human-GI AI via P214-Z / P218-E ACL only — `no_module_local_llm`.
6. Collective decisions and ethical intelligence require Workflow + Policy + Identity — never ungated collective decision; never skip cognitive privacy; never violate cognitive sovereignty; human authority preserved.
7. Human evolution capability expansion via P218-U; civilization context via P218-T; education knowledge via P218-S — never replace those platforms.
8. Never replace Core, AI, Quantum, Robotics, Biotechnology, prior P218 fabrics (including P218-U human evolution).
9. Foundation for P218-W.

## Consequences

Positive: cognitive foundation for advanced human intelligence civilization and collective reasoning.  
Negative: Human-GI operations must stay aligned with Evolution (P218-U), Civilization (P218-T), Education (P218-S) and cognitive sovereignty contracts.

## Alternatives rejected

- Sibling `human_gi` BC outside SoR space.
- Ungated collective civilization decisions without ethical review.
- Module-local LLM for reasoning enhancement.
