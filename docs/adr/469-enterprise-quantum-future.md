# ADR 469: Enterprise Quantum Future Architecture & MEOS Ultimate Intelligence Evolution Fabric (P215-X)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-01
- **Capability:** `CAP-PLT-QC-001`
- **Deciders:** Chief Enterprise Architect, MEOS EAGS 11.0

## Context

P215-W delivers civilization intelligence. P215-X must provide the **future evolution foundation**—post-QGI expansion, singularity readiness modeling, architecture simulation, and ultimate intelligence expansion—without replacing W/V/U/T or Core, and without ungoverned singularity acceleration.

## Decision

1. Extend SoR `quantum` with fabric `meos_ultimate_intelligence_evolution_fabric` at `/api/v1/quantum/future*`.
2. Six BCs (architecture, post-QGI, singularity, scenario, expansion, evolution governance).
3. AI via P214-Z / AI Platform ACL only; KG via Search; twin via platform twin contracts; approvals via Workflow; audit via Audit Platform.
4. Forbid sibling `quantum_future_*` contexts and local LLM/metrics stores.
5. Singularity modeling is readiness/risk/forecast only—never ungated self-improvement loops.

## Consequences

- Positive: Governed long-horizon evolution beyond civilization baseline.
- Negative: Expansion depends on W/V/U/T maturity and Policy/Workflow capacity.
- Compliance: SECURITY_STANDARD, ENTERPRISE_EVENT_BUS, AI_PLATFORM_STANDARD, DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL.
