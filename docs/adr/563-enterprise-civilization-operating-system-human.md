# ADR 563 — Enterprise Civilization Operating System Human Civilization Intelligence (P219-J)

## Status
Accepted

## Context
P219-I established knowledge civilization. P219-J establishes Global Human Intelligence Platform, Human Digital Twin, Human Development Intelligence, Workforce Intelligence and MEOS Human Civilization Intelligence Core before P219-K civilization governance intelligence.

## Decision
1. Fabric `meos_civilization_os_human_civilization_intelligence_framework`; API `/api/v1/civilization/human*`.
2. Evolution: Human Records → Information → Intelligence → Digital Twin → Development Intelligence → Adaptive Civilization Intelligence.
3. Four BCs: Human Intelligence Core, Human Digital Twin, Learning Intelligence, Workforce Intelligence.
4. Never replace Identity — store identity_ref only; auth/identity lifecycle remains Identity SoR.
5. Privacy by design mandatory; never ungated human profile mutation; twin sync/simulation workflow- and policy-gated.
6. Inference via P214-Z; human reasoning via P219-E; human knowledge via P219-I; human capital via P219-H.
7. Never replace P219–P219-I or peer SoRs; never violate human sovereignty.
8. Foundation for P219-K civilization governance intelligence.

## Consequences
Positive: governed human intelligence fabric before governance phase.  
Negative: profile/twin mutations remain workflow- and policy-gated; no local identity stack.
