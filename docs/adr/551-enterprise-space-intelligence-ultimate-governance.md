# ADR 551 — Enterprise Space Intelligence Ultimate Intelligence Governance (P218-Y)

## Status

Accepted

## Context

P218-X established Civilization Singularity Intelligence. P218-Y defines Ultimate Intelligence Governance spanning alignment civilization, future trust architecture, advanced ethics and intelligence safety — before Final Intelligence Nexus (P218-Z).

## Decision

1. SoR remains `space` / `CAP-PLT-SP-001`.
2. Fabric: `meos_ultimate_intelligence_governance_fabric`.
3. API: `/api/v1/space/ultimate-governance*`.
4. Five ultimate governance layers; core + alignment + trust + ethics + safety catalogs.
5. All governance AI via P214-Z / P218-E ACL only — `no_module_local_llm`.
6. Governance decisions and alignment require Workflow + Policy + Identity — never ungated governance decision; never skip intelligence alignment; never violate human sovereignty; human authority preserved.
7. Singularity via P218-X; Collective SI via P218-W; Human-GI via P218-V; human evolution via P218-U; civilization via P218-T — never replace those platforms.
8. Never replace Core, AI, Quantum, Robotics, Biotechnology, prior P218 fabrics (including P218-X Singularity).
9. Foundation for P218-Z.

## Consequences

Positive: governance, ethics and trust foundation required before ultimate intelligence civilization systems.  
Negative: ultimate governance operations must stay aligned with Singularity (P218-X), Collective SI (P218-W) and sovereignty/alignment contracts.

## Alternatives rejected

- Sibling `ultimate_governance` BC outside SoR space.
- Ungated advanced intelligence policy without ethical validation.
- Module-local LLM for alignment evaluation.
