**SoR:** `ai` · **ADR:** 422 · **API:** `/api/v1/ai/mission*` · **Capability:** `CAP-PLT-AI-001`

## Mission

**Enterprise AI SHALL transform MEOS from a traditional enterprise platform into an adaptive, intelligent, autonomous and continuously learning operating system.**

## Vision

**MEOS Enterprise AI SHALL become the intelligence layer that continuously learns, reasons, predicts, optimizes and improves every enterprise capability.**

MEOS Enterprise AI Strategic Intelligence Framework — business strategy + enterprise data + knowledge + AI capabilities + human expertise + autonomous intelligence → transformation → intelligent operations → strategic decision advantage → autonomous capabilities → continuous evolution.
