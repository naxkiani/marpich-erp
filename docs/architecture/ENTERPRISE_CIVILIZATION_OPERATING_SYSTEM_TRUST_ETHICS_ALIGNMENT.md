## Principle

Design the complete Enterprise Trust, Ethics & Alignment Platform that enables MEOS to provide trustworthy, transparent, explainable and policy-aligned enterprise operations across all domains — supporting governance, auditability, compliance and human oversight for AI-assisted enterprise decision support. The platform does not replace institutional governance, compliance SoR or human accountability. Emphasize trust-by-design, explainability and continuous assurance rather than opaque autonomous control.
