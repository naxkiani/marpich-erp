Establish the Unified Enterprise Control Plane that integrates enterprise intelligence, cross-domain orchestration, governance fabric, digital twin federation and strategic decision support into MEOS Unified Enterprise Core — under human authority, policy alignment and MEOS standards. Emphasize unified visibility and orchestrated decision support rather than centralized autonomous control of the enterprise.
