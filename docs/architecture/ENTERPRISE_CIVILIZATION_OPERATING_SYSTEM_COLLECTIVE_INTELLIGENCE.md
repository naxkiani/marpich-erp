## Principle

Create a civilization-scale collaboration platform capable of integrating distributed expertise, institutional knowledge and AI-assisted analysis into coordinated strategic execution — under human-centered collaboration, federated coordination, explainable decision support and MEOS standards. The platform coordinates distributed expertise and information; it does not replace institutional governance or human accountability. Emphasize knowledge sharing, coordination and explainable collaboration rather than centralized autonomous control. Never replace P219-P Collaboration, P219-V Gen Intel or P219-I Knowledge.
